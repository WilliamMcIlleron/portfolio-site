Questions

Before you book

Anything else, WhatsApp us on 010 000 0000.

Do I need a consultation before my first facial?

No. Every booking includes a short skin form, and we read it with you before we start. Book the consultation if you would like a full plan first.

What does "from" mean on the prices?

It is the price of the treatment as listed. If we suggest adding something on the day, we tell you the price before we do it, so there are no surprises on the bill.

Can I book if I am pregnant?

Yes, for most facials and massage. Peels are not offered during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Tick the box on the skin form and we will plan around it.

How do I cancel or move a booking?

WhatsApp us at least 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours we may charge half the treatment price.

What should I do before I come in?

Come with a clean face if you can, and stop retinoids three days before a peel. Arrive ten minutes early to settle in.
