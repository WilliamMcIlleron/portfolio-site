Our story

Small on purpose.

Soft Light is a two-room studio for people who want their skin looked after without the hard sell. Every treatment starts with a conversation, and nothing goes on your skin until you know what it is and why.

We keep the shelf short: eight products we use ourselves in the treatment room. If one is not right for you, we will say so.

Soft Light Skin Studio is fictional. This page shows how a real studio can tell its story in a few honest lines.

Questions

Good to know

Where do I park?

Behind the building, in the bays marked for the studio. It is free. The gate code comes with your booking confirmation.

Is the studio step-free?

Yes. Both treatment rooms are on the ground floor and the entrance has no steps.

How can I pay?

Card, tap or Instant EFT at the studio after your treatment.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, for any amount or treatment. Ask at reception or on WhatsApp.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes. There is a small waiting area with tea, and we can book two treatments back to back.
