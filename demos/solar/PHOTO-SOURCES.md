# Photo sources: Brightline Solar demo

Brightline Solar is a made-up business. Every photo below is a real photograph
(none are AI-generated) published on Wikimedia Commons as **public domain**
(mostly United States government works) or **CC0**. Both allow commercial use
without permission. Credit is given anyway, here and in the site footer.

Processing, applied the same way to every image: one shared grade (slightly
warmer, saturation 0.93, contrast 1.05), resized to three widths, saved as
WebP. Three edits beyond the grade:

- `installer-panel`: stickers on the hard hat blurred so no brand is readable.
- `ground-grass`: the inverter's maker label blurred.
- `house-two-roofs`: a porch flag painted out with the neighbouring siding.

Unsplash and Pexels were the preferred sources, but both refused automated
access from this machine (HTTP 403 and a bot challenge), so nothing was taken
from them.
