01What we found and what we propose

Your 12-month average bill is R3,400 (about 1,000 kWh a month). The main roof is concrete tile, facing north at about 26 degrees, with no shading between 08:00 and 16:00. Your DB board has space for a separate essentials circuit.

We propose 12 panels on the north roof, an 8 kW hybrid inverter and 15 kWh of lithium battery in the garage. In an outage the essentials circuit keeps running: lights, Wi-Fi, fridge, TV, home office, gate, alarm, kettle and microwave. The geyser, stove and oven stay on grid power only.

07Key terms, in plain language

  1. The price is fixed. It changes only if you sign a written change order, for example to add a battery.
  2. Changing your mind. Cancel within 5 working days of signing and the deposit comes back in full. After equipment is ordered, we refund it less any supplier restocking fee, with the supplier's invoice.
  3. Ownership. The equipment is yours once the final payment clears. Until then it remains ours.
  4. Your roof. We report any existing damage at the site visit and before we start. We are responsible for every hole we make, for 10 years.
  5. Access. You give us access to the roof, the DB board and the garage on the agreed days.
  6. Insurance. Tell your insurer about the system before installation. We send you the CoC and spec sheet for them.
  7. Disputes. We try to sort it out directly first, in writing, within 10 working days.
