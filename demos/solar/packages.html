Packages

Three starting points.

Every system is sized to your house after a site visit. These show what each kind of system usually looks like and what it costs installed, so you know where you stand before we talk.

Backup

Essentials

R69,000 to R134,000Incl. VAT, installed. 5 kWh with 4 panels at the low end, 10 kWh with 6 panels at the high end.

Included

  • 5 kW hybrid inverter
  • 5 or 10 kWh lithium battery
  • 4 to 6 panels (2.2 to 3.3 kWp)
  • Essentials circuit split at your DB board
  • CoC, registration and handover

Runs in an outage

Lights, Wi-Fi, fridge, TV, laptops, gate and alarm. About 14 hours of that on 10 kWh.

Suits

Homes that mainly want outages to stop mattering, on a smaller budget.

Get a quote for this

Hybrid

Bill and backup

R125,000 to R174,000Incl. VAT, installed, for the configuration below.

Included

  • 8 kW hybrid inverter
  • 10 kWh lithium battery
  • 12 panels (6.6 kWp)
  • Monitoring app set up on your phone
  • CoC, registration and handover

Runs

Most of the house by day from the sun. In an outage, everything except the geyser, stove and oven.

Suits

Families with a R2,000 to R4,000 bill who want it lower and want backup too.

Get a quote for this

Off-grid

No grid

R275,000 to R380,000Incl. VAT, installed, for the configuration below. Plots vary most.

Included

  • 16 kW off-grid inverter
  • 30 kWh lithium battery
  • 24 panels (13.2 kWp), roof or ground
  • Generator input and changeover
  • CoC and handover

Runs

A whole house plus a borehole pump, through the night and a cloudy morning.

Suits

Plots and farms where the grid is absent, unreliable or very expensive to connect.
