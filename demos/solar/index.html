What we fit

Four kinds of system. One honest answer about which you need.

  1. 01

    Backup

    Inverter and battery for the essentials, with a few panels to recharge it. The cheapest way to stop outages running your evening.

    Runs: lights, Wi-Fi, fridge, TV, gate and alarm.

  2. 02

    Hybrid

    Panels, inverter and battery working together. Cuts the bill by day and carries the house through an outage.

    Runs: most of the house, minus the geyser and oven.

  3. 03

    Grid-tied

    Panels and an inverter, no battery. The fastest payback, but it switches off when the grid does.

    Runs: daytime use: pool pump, aircon, office.

  4. 04

    Off-grid

    For plots and farms with no reliable supply. Sized to your real daily use, with a generator input for long cloudy spells.

    Runs: everything you plan for, on its own.

Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else, ask on the quote form or call 010 000 0000.

Will it keep the lights on during load-shedding?

A system with a battery will, for the circuits it is wired to. We split your essentials onto their own circuit so the battery is not drained by the geyser, stove or oven. Grid-tied systems without a battery switch off when the grid goes down; that is a safety rule, not a fault.

How big a battery do I need?

It depends on what must run and for how long. Lights, Wi-Fi, a fridge and a TV draw roughly 400 to 600 W together, so a 5 kWh battery covers about 7 to 11 hours of that. A pool pump or kettle changes the answer, which is why we size from your list, not a package name. The calculator shows the maths.

Can you fit panels on my roof type?

Concrete and clay tile, corrugated and IBR sheeting, and flat concrete roofs all work, each with its own fixings. Tile roofs use hooks under the tiles; sheeting uses clamps on the profile; flat roofs use weighted tilt frames. Slate and thatch need a ground mount instead. We check the roof structure on the site visit.

Do I need municipal approval?

Yes. In Johannesburg, Tshwane and Ekurhuleni a grid-connected system must be registered with the municipality, and every installation needs an electrical Certificate of Compliance. We do the paperwork and include it in the price. Rules and fees differ by municipality, so the proposal lists what applies to your address.

Will my home insurance cover the system?

Usually, once you tell your insurer and send them the CoC. Most add the system to the buildings cover, sometimes with a small premium increase. Please check with your own insurer before installation; we supply the documents they ask for.

How long until it pays for itself?

Our estimates for Gauteng homes mostly land between 4 and 8 years, depending on the system, your bill and how much of the solar you use yourself. That is an estimate, not a promise: tariffs, weather and habits all move it. The calculator lists every assumption.
