The formulas

  1. Monthly use: bill / R3.40 kWh. Daily use: that divided by 30.4.
  2. Solar target: daily use times 30% (backup), 70% (cut the bill), 80% (both) or 125% (off-grid, for cloudy days).
  3. Panels: target / (5.2 h x direction x 0.80) kW, rounded up to whole 550 W panels (4 to 40).
  4. Battery: essentials watts x 4 h / 0.9; "both" adds 25% of daily use; off-grid holds 60% of daily use for 1.5 days; "cut the bill" gets one 5 kWh module. Rounded up to 5 kWh.
  5. Inverter: the next standard size above both 1.25 x the peak of your essentials and panel kW / 1.3.
  6. Price: panels at R3,250 to R4,250 each fitted (roof type adjusts the fitting), inverter at R3,300 to R4,700 per kW, battery at R4,200 to R5,900 per kWh, plus R18,000 to R26,000 for protection, wiring, CoC and paperwork. Incl. VAT.
  7. Saving: solar made x the share you use yourself (80% to 100%, no feed-in credit) x R3.40, never more than your bill less fixed charges. Shown as 88% to 105% of that.
  8. Payback: the year the running total of savings (rising 8% a year, less 0.5% panel ageing) passes the price.
