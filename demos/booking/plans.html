Plans and prices

Pick by pool, not by guesswork.

Every plan includes the full clean, the water test and the chemicals. The price depends on your pool's size. Not sure which fits? The quiz picks for you in about a minute.

Find my plan →

01

Weekly Care

For family pools and large pools. Hard use and more water mean it needs checking every week.

  • 4 visits a month on the same weekday
  • Skim, brush, vacuum and empty the baskets
  • Water tested and balanced, chemicals included
  • A photo and the test results on WhatsApp after every visit

A month, by size

  • PlungeR1,150 to R1,350
  • StandardR1,450 to R1,650
  • LargeR1,850 to R2,150

02

Fortnightly Care

For plunge and standard pools used now and then. A visit every second week keeps it balanced.

  • 2 visits a month, every second week
  • The same full clean and water test
  • A top-up dose to carry the water through the gap
  • You skim leaves between visits if the trees drop a lot

A month, by size

  • PlungeR650 to R800
  • StandardR850 to R1,000
  • LargeR1,150 to R1,350

03

Handover and Care

For new pools. The first 3 months need gentle chemistry and careful brushing while the surface cures.

  • A start-up visit: chemistry set, pump and timer explained
  • Weekly visits for the first 3 months
  • Brushing that protects a curing marbelite finish
  • Then you move to Weekly or Fortnightly at that plan's price

A month for 3 months

  • PlungeR1,250 to R1,450
  • StandardR1,550 to R1,750
  • LargeR1,950 to R2,250

Sizes: plunge is up to about 20,000 litres, standard about 20,000 to 50,000, large over 50,000. The top of each range is for salt pools (the cell needs cleaning every 3 months) and gardens with heavy leaf fall.

About this demo

A quiz funnel swaps a long form for a few easy taps, so more visitors finish and each one sees a plan and a price made for them. Every answer arrives with the booking, so the owner knows the pool before calling back. William McIlleron built this to show what a booking system on your own site can do. See the booking service
