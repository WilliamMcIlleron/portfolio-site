FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need to be home?

No. If we can get to the pool, we do the visit and send you a photo and the test results on WhatsApp. Most customers leave a gate code with us.

Are chemicals included?

Yes. The normal chemicals for balancing and sanitising are in every plan. A Reset includes the extra shock and floc. Salt for a salt pool is quoted separately when it is needed.

Is there a contract?

No. You pay monthly and can stop or pause with one WhatsApp message.

What if it rains on my day?

Light rain, we still come. If a storm makes it unsafe, we move you to the next free day and tell you before the time.

Why does the quiz ask so much?

So the first call is a quick confirmation, not an interview. The technician arrives knowing your pool, the water and what you want, with the right chemicals in the bakkie.

When do I pay?

After the first visit. Nothing is charged when you book.
